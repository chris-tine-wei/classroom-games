Christine Peng · Side builds

Three games I built for my ESL class

Built for three students aged 10 to 14, taught online over five months. Each game covers the vocabulary from one chapter of the book we read together. Each one is a single HTML file with no dependencies, so it runs offline in any browser.

Quidditch Jeopardy

Team quiz · Chapter 11

A 20-clue board with Daily Double wagers, a countdown timer and a podium at the end. I built a new board for 10 of the 11 chapters we covered.

Tomodachi Hogwarts

Story in scenes · Chapter 10

Fifteen words told through five scenes from the chapter. Two students take turns, with a hint, a chapter clue, and a quick-fire round at the end.

Remembrall Dive Race

Timed race · Chapter 9

The whole class races the clock to match 14 definitions. The sky turns from afternoon to midnight as the chapter moves on.
