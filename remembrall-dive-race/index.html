Ch.9 Remembrall Dive Race

The Midnight Duel · 14 Sky Blocks · One Class Score

Share your screen. The Remembrall glows and shows a definition from Chapter 9. Students shout the word out loud or type it in chat. You click the matching sky block.

Right block → the class dives, catches the word, and the score goes up.
Wrong block → the block cracks, the class loses 3 points, and the streak resets.
25 seconds per word. Catch it in the first 10 seconds for a speed bonus.

One board, one run. The words arrive in the order the chapter does, and the sky above the class burns down from afternoon to midnight as the flying lesson turns into the duel.
Everyone is on the same team. One score, 14 words, no losers.
